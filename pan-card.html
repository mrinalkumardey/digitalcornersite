Common questions

What can I correct on my PAN card?

You can correct or update your name, date of birth, father's name, address, photo and signature. Tell us what needs to change and we will confirm the documents.

I lost my PAN card. Can you help?

Yes. Share your Aadhaar and date of birth and we will help find your PAN and get a copy.

What is PAN surrender?

A person should hold only one PAN. If you have more than one by mistake, the extra PAN has to be surrendered. Message us before you use or cancel any of them.

Can a child get a PAN card?

Yes. A PAN can be applied for a minor through a parent or guardian, and for adults aged 18 and above.
