Birth Certificate

1 service

Documents needed

  • Parent's Aadhaar
  • Child's Aadhaar, PAN, any age proof or medical certificate
  • Application form
  • Child above 6 years: school certificate or admit card. Child under 6 years: parent's Aadhaar
  • Legacy voter copy, Gaon Pradhan certificate, or voter details as other documents
  • Affidavit sworn before a Magistrate

Caste Certificate

1 service

Documents needed

  • Aadhaar
  • Photo
  • Caste linkage through father, uncle or brother (the family tree must be proven)
  • No caste linkage: recommendation letter from the respective caste board or committee
  • Gaon Buha certificate stating caste and permanent residence
