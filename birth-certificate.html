Common questions

Which documents do I need for a birth certificate?

The list is on this page. It depends on the child's age, and we confirm it on WhatsApp before you come.

What if the child is above 6 years?

For a child above 6 years, bring the school certificate or admit card. For a child under 6 years, the parent's Aadhaar is used.

Do I need an affidavit?

The list includes an affidavit sworn before a Magistrate. We tell you whether your case needs one.

Not sure which one you need?

Tell us what you are trying to get done. We will tell you the right service and the documents to bring.
